and have been the:

Passions

Music is one of the biggest passions; I enjoy listening to, interpreting, and playing music whenever I get the chance.

The saxophone is my musical instrument of choice and I've previously played tenor with the Andrews University Wind Symphony.

I occasionally record some of my practice sessions and post short visuals to accompany my recordings on YouTube with the intention of documenting my progress. You can see my first video here and I'm hoping to start posting on a more regular basis soon.
